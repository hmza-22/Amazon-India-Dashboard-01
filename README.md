# Amazon-India-Dashboard-01
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing Amazon India sales data - tracks overall sales &amp; profit performance, category/product trends, order status &amp; returns, and payment/fulfillment/geographic breakdowns using PivotTables and PivotCharts.

**Amazon India Sales Dashboard**

An Excel-based interactive dashboard built to analyze Amazon India sales, profit, and order data, using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers to answer key business questions across four dashboard views.

**Dashboards**

1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance
Tracks Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Units Sold, Average Order Value, Profit Margin %, and the Monthly Sales & Profit Trend — giving a quick read on whether the business is growing or declining over time.

2. Category & Product Performance
Breaks down Category-wise Sales, Profit, and Units Sold, plus a Top 10 Products by Sales view — identifying high-performing categories and products that need attention.

3. Order Status & Revenue Loss
Shows Delivered, Shipped, Returned, and Cancelled order counts along with Return Rate % and Cancellation Rate %, and Category-wise Returns & Cancellations — surfacing how much revenue is being lost to returns and cancellations.

4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance
Analyzes Sales and Profit by Payment Method and Fulfillment Method, and State-wise Sales, Profit, and Orders — showing which payment channels, fulfillment types, and regions drive the most business.

**Tools Used**
Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Data Cleaning)
